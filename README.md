Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in
desktop Chrome. No build step, no network needed — everything is vendored
under `game/`.

## The game

Neon Harbor is a living harbor city: around ninety pedestrians going about
their day — café regulars, dock workers, joggers, buskers — and more than
fifty vehicles on the road. You play a driver caught between a three-mission
story chain for Marisol Vega, twelve hidden shells scattered across the
districts, and a police force that answers with patrol cars, an Interceptor
GT pursuit car and an AIR-1 helicopter. Your own ride is the VANTA GT
supercar. Touch controls are included for phones; the soundtrack is
synthesized live with WebAudio.
